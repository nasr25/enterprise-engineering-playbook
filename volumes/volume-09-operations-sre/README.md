# Volume 09 — Operations & SRE

Enterprise standards for reliable production operations, service ownership, observability, SLI/SLO management, incident response, capacity, resilience, continuity, runbooks, and production readiness.

## Scope

This volume applies to web applications, APIs, background workers, databases, integration services, mobile backends, infrastructure, platforms, and internal enterprise systems in cloud, on-premises, hybrid, and isolated environments.

## Chapters

1. Service Ownership and Reliability Governance — `SRE-001`–`SRE-020`
2. SLI, SLO, SLA, and Error Budgets — `SRE-021`–`SRE-040`
3. Observability and Alerting — `SRE-041`–`SRE-065`
4. Incident Response and Problem Management — `SRE-066`–`SRE-090`
5. Capacity, Performance, and Reliability Engineering — `SRE-091`–`SRE-115`
6. Backup, Disaster Recovery, and Continuity — `SRE-116`–`SRE-140`
7. Runbooks, Operations, and Change Safety — `SRE-141`–`SRE-160`
8. Production Readiness and Review Checklist — `SRE-161`–`SRE-180`

## Core Principle

Production reliability is an engineering responsibility, not an afterthought. Every service must have explicit ownership, measurable objectives, safe failure behavior, observability, operational procedures, and evidence that recovery works.
