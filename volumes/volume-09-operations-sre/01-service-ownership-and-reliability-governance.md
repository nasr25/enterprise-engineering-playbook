# Service Ownership and Reliability Governance

- **SRE-001 — Named Service Owner:** Every production service MUST have a named technical owner and business/service owner where applicable.
- **SRE-002 — Service Catalog Entry:** Production services MUST be registered with purpose, dependencies, criticality, contacts, and support model.
- **SRE-003 — Criticality Classification:** Services MUST be classified by business impact, recovery need, data sensitivity, and dependency importance.
- **SRE-004 — Support Coverage:** Required support hours and escalation expectations MUST match service criticality.
- **SRE-005 — Operational Acceptance:** New services MUST pass operational readiness review before production ownership is accepted.
- **SRE-006 — Ownership Transfer:** Ownership changes MUST transfer documentation, access, alerts, dashboards, runbooks, and open risks.
- **SRE-007 — Dependency Ownership:** Critical upstream and downstream dependencies MUST have identifiable owners or support paths.
- **SRE-008 — Reliability Requirements:** Availability, latency, throughput, recovery, and durability expectations MUST be defined for critical services.
- **SRE-009 — Reliability Risk Register:** Material reliability risks MUST be tracked with owner, mitigation, and target date.
- **SRE-010 — Toil Identification:** Repetitive manual operational work SHOULD be measured and prioritized for elimination or automation.
- **SRE-011 — Automation Safety:** Operational automation MUST include validation, bounded scope, logging, and safe failure behavior.
- **SRE-012 — Privileged Operations:** Production administrative access MUST follow least privilege, traceability, and approval requirements.
- **SRE-013 — Operational Documentation:** Service documentation MUST reflect actual deployed topology and operational dependencies.
- **SRE-014 — Runbook Ownership:** Every critical runbook MUST have an owner and review date.
- **SRE-015 — Maintenance Windows:** Planned maintenance expectations MUST be defined and communicated where user impact is possible.
- **SRE-016 — Operational Change Records:** Material production changes MUST be traceable to an approved change or release record.
- **SRE-017 — Risk-Based Controls:** Operational controls MAY vary by criticality, but higher criticality MUST never receive weaker evidence requirements.
- **SRE-018 — Lifecycle State:** Services MUST have explicit lifecycle states such as experimental, production, deprecated, and retired.
- **SRE-019 — Retirement:** Service retirement MUST remove traffic, dependencies, credentials, alerts, dashboards, jobs, and obsolete infrastructure safely.
- **SRE-020 — Periodic Reliability Review:** Critical services MUST be periodically reviewed for ownership, SLO health, incident patterns, capacity, and recovery readiness.
