# Backup, Disaster Recovery, and Continuity

- **SRE-116 — Recovery Objectives:** Critical services MUST define RTO and RPO aligned with business impact.
- **SRE-117 — Backup Scope:** Backup scope MUST include required data, configuration, secrets metadata, certificates, application state, and recovery documentation as applicable.
- **SRE-118 — Backup Frequency:** Backup frequency MUST be sufficient to meet the approved RPO.
- **SRE-119 — Backup Retention:** Backup retention MUST satisfy recovery, legal, security, and business requirements.
- **SRE-120 — Backup Encryption:** Sensitive backups MUST be encrypted in transit and at rest.
- **SRE-121 — Backup Access:** Backup systems MUST use least privilege and separate administrative access from routine application access.
- **SRE-122 — Immutable or Protected Copies:** Critical backups SHOULD include immutable, offline, or otherwise protected copies resistant to destructive compromise.
- **SRE-123 — Geographic or Failure-Domain Separation:** Critical recovery copies SHOULD be isolated from the same failure domain as primary systems.
- **SRE-124 — Restore Testing:** Backups MUST be periodically restored and validated; successful backup jobs alone are insufficient evidence.
- **SRE-125 — Restore Completeness:** Recovery tests MUST verify application usability and data integrity, not merely file restoration.
- **SRE-126 — Dependency Recovery:** DR plans MUST include critical infrastructure, identity, DNS, networking, certificates, integrations, databases, queues, and external dependencies.
- **SRE-127 — Recovery Order:** Complex services MUST document dependency-aware recovery order.
- **SRE-128 — Failover Procedure:** Critical services MUST define failover activation criteria, authority, and operating procedure.
- **SRE-129 — Failback Procedure:** Services using failover MUST define safe failback and reconciliation procedures.
- **SRE-130 — Data Reconciliation:** Recovery after asynchronous replication or partial processing MUST define how missing, duplicated, or divergent data is detected and reconciled.
- **SRE-131 — DR Environment Readiness:** Standby environments MUST be maintained sufficiently to meet the declared RTO.
- **SRE-132 — Configuration Parity:** Recovery environments MUST have controlled, documented configuration differences from primary production.
- **SRE-133 — DR Exercise:** Critical systems MUST periodically execute realistic recovery exercises.
- **SRE-134 — Scenario Diversity:** Exercises SHOULD cover infrastructure loss, database loss, dependency failure, credential compromise, and operator unavailability as relevant.
- **SRE-135 — Business Continuity:** Technical recovery plans MUST align with business continuity procedures and manual workarounds where required.
- **SRE-136 — Communication Plan:** Major recovery events MUST define operational, management, stakeholder, and vendor communication paths.
- **SRE-137 — Recovery Access:** Emergency access required for recovery MUST be tested and securely controlled.
- **SRE-138 — Recovery Evidence:** DR exercises MUST capture actual recovery times, data loss, failures, lessons, and corrective actions.
- **SRE-139 — DR Change Review:** Material architecture, storage, identity, network, or dependency changes MUST trigger review of recovery procedures.
- **SRE-140 — Continuity Review:** RTO, RPO, backups, recovery dependencies, and exercise outcomes MUST be periodically reviewed by accountable owners.
