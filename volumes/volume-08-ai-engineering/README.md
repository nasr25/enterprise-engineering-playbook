# Volume 08 — AI Engineering

Enterprise standards for designing, building, integrating, evaluating, securing, deploying, and operating AI-enabled systems.

## Scope

This volume is model-, vendor-, and cloud-neutral. It applies to predictive ML, deep learning, NLP, computer vision, generative AI, LLM applications, RAG, agents, and AI-assisted enterprise workflows, including fully on-premises and isolated environments.

## Chapters

1. AI Governance and Risk — `AI-001`–`AI-020`
2. Data and ML Lifecycle — `AI-021`–`AI-040`
3. Model Development and Evaluation — `AI-041`–`AI-060`
4. Generative AI, LLM, and RAG — `AI-061`–`AI-085`
5. AI Security and Privacy — `AI-086`–`AI-110`
6. MLOps, Deployment, and Operations — `AI-111`–`AI-135`
7. Agents, Tools, and Human Oversight — `AI-136`–`AI-155`
8. Responsible AI and Compliance — `AI-156`–`AI-170`
9. AI Engineering Review Checklist — `AI-171`–`AI-180`

## Core Principle

AI components are production software components. They must satisfy the same architecture, security, privacy, testing, observability, change-management, and operational controls as conventional software, plus AI-specific controls for data provenance, model behavior, evaluation, misuse, and human oversight.
