# Responsible AI and Compliance

- **AI-156 — Applicable Obligations:** AI systems MUST identify applicable laws, regulations, contracts, internal policies, and sector requirements.
- **AI-157 — Prohibited Uses:** Prohibited or unacceptable AI uses MUST be documented and technically or procedurally prevented where feasible.
- **AI-158 — Transparency:** Users SHOULD be informed when they are materially interacting with AI or AI-generated content where context requires it.
- **AI-159 — Human Review Disclosure:** Systems MUST NOT imply human review occurred when a decision or response was solely automated.
- **AI-160 — Fairness Assessment:** Use cases affecting people SHOULD assess material unfairness or disparate performance risks relevant to the domain.
- **AI-161 — Accessibility:** AI-enabled user experiences MUST follow applicable accessibility requirements.
- **AI-162 — Contestability:** High-impact AI-supported decisions SHOULD provide a path for review, correction, or appeal where appropriate.
- **AI-163 — Explainability:** Explanations MUST be truthful about what the system can establish and MUST NOT invent causal reasoning.
- **AI-164 — Intellectual Property:** Training, retrieval, generation, and publication workflows MUST consider intellectual-property and licensing obligations.
- **AI-165 — Content Provenance:** AI-generated media SHOULD support provenance or labeling controls where misuse or authenticity is a material risk.
- **AI-166 — Record Keeping:** Required AI governance, evaluation, approvals, incidents, and change records MUST be retained according to policy.
- **AI-167 — User Feedback Channel:** Production AI SHOULD provide a mechanism to report harmful, incorrect, or inappropriate behavior when user-facing.
- **AI-168 — Misuse Monitoring:** Material abuse patterns SHOULD be monitored while respecting privacy and lawful-use constraints.
- **AI-169 — Periodic Control Review:** Responsible-AI controls MUST be reviewed as models, laws, risks, and use cases evolve.
- **AI-170 — Evidence Over Claims:** Compliance or safety claims MUST be supported by documented controls and test evidence, not vendor marketing alone.
